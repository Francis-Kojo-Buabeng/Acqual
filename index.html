    • Emmanuel Acqual's Work on Picture Frames

    A picture frame is a decorative border used to enhance and protect artwork, photographs, or prints. Frames come in various materials, such as wood, metal, or plastic, and can range from simple designs to ornate styles. They not only preserve the image but also add aesthetic value, complementing home or gallery decor. A well-chosen frame can highlight the beauty of the picture while blending seamlessly with its surroundings.
